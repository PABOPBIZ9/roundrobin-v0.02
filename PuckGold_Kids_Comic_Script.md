# PUCKGOLD KIDZ
## An Original All-Ages Comic Starring the Founding Four Mascots
### Issue #1: "The Golden Puck"

*Note: this is an original concept and script — its own characters, world, and story — not a reproduction of any existing comic property. Think of it as your own collectible-mascot universe built around the PuckGold teams, written for kids roughly ages 5–10.*

---

## THE CONCEPT

Each PuckGold team gets a kid-friendly mascot character — small, big-eyed, big-hearted versions of the team identity — who go on adventures together that teach a simple value (teamwork, courage, honesty, effort). Every issue ends with a short "Locker Room Lesson" page, a recurring feature that spells out the value in plain language for young readers, plus a mini activity (word search, "spot the difference," etc.).

This is built to be collectible: trading cards, plush toys, and a growing roster of characters as the league expands — the same way sports-mascot-universe properties work.

---

## MEET THE CHARACTERS

### 🦎 GECKO GUS — Miami Mighty Geckz
A small, bright turquoise gecko in a tiny orange jersey. Loves speed, never sits still, talks fast. His catchphrase: **"Quick feet, big heart!"** Character trait: **Perseverance** — Gus never gives up, even when he's behind.

### 🐦 CARDY — McLean Cardinals
A sharp, tidy little red cardinal who always has a plan. Wears a tiny gold whistle around his neck like a junior coach. Catchphrase: **"Practice makes perfect!"** Character trait: **Discipline** — Cardy believes in doing things the right way, every time.

### 🏛️ DOME-STER — Washington Whoompt
A round, friendly navy-and-silver dome-shaped little guy with tiny arms and legs, always thinking three steps ahead. Catchphrase: **"Let's think it through!"** Character trait: **Smart teamwork** — Dome-ster is the strategist of the group.

### 🚂 CHUGGY — Chattanooga Choo Choo
A small steel-gray train-engine character with a big friendly headlight-eye and a puff of steam for hair. Catchphrase: **"All aboard for effort!"** Character trait: **Heart & hustle** — Chuggy isn't the fastest or fanciest, but he never stops trying.

## ISSUE #1: "THE GOLDEN PUCK"

**Page 1 — Cover**
*Big, colorful splash: Gecko Gus, Cardy, Dome-ster, and Chuggy standing together on a sparkling ice rink under four spotlights, a glowing golden puck floating in the middle. Title lettering: "PUCKGOLD KIDZ: The Golden Puck — Issue #1."*

**Page 2–3 — The Setup**
*Panel 1:* Four little rinks light up one by one — Neon Reef, Cardinal Perch, District Dome, Terminal Arena — each with its mascot practicing.
*Panel 2:* A narrator caption: "Four teams. Four homes. One big weekend to see who's really got the most heart."
*Panel 3:* Gecko Gus zooms across the ice, trips over his own skates, and lands in a heap. He pops back up laughing. "Quick feet, big heart — even when the feet trip!"

**Page 4–5 — Game Day Jitters**
*Panel 1:* Cardy lines up cones in a perfect row before the game, checking them twice.
*Panel 2:* Chuggy chugs by, a little slower, a little out of breath, but grinning. "Doesn't matter how fast — I'm still coming!"
*Panel 3:* Dome-ster taps his head. "I've got a plan for every play. Teamwork makes it work!"
*Panel 4:* All four mascots wave to the crowd of little fans in the stands.

**Page 6–8 — The Big Game**
*Panel 1:* The game starts! Quick, kid-friendly action panels — no real violence, just fun cartoon hockey (big smiles, bouncy motion lines, a comically oversized puck).
*Panel 2:* Gecko Gus falls behind early, feeling discouraged. He sits by the boards.
*Panel 3:* Chuggy skates over. "Hey — everybody has a slow start sometimes. Wanna try again together?"
*Panel 4:* Gus smiles, gets back up. "Yeah! Let's go!"

**Page 9–10 — The Comeback**
*Panel 1:* Gus, cheered on by all three other mascots (even though they're "opponents" — everyone's still friends), skates his fastest lap yet.
*Panel 2:* He scores a big, silly, celebratory goal — confetti, cheering, everybody laughing together.
*Panel 3:* The golden puck glows and floats up above the rink, and all four mascots gather underneath it, holding hands/paws/wings.

**Page 11 — The Ending**
*Caption:* "In PuckGold, everybody gets a golden moment — because trying your best IS the win."
*Final panel:* All four mascots sharing the golden trophy, taking turns holding it, laughing together.

**Page 12 — "Locker Room Lesson"**
> **Today's Lesson: It's okay to fall down — what matters is getting back up.**
> Did you ever feel like Gecko Gus, a little behind everyone else? What's one small step you can take today to keep trying?

**Page 13 — Activity Page**
- Word search: GUS, CARDY, DOME-STER, CHUGGY, PUCK, TEAM, HEART, TRY
- "Color the Golden Puck" coloring panel
- "Which mascot are you most like?" quiz (3 simple questions)

## SERIES OUTLINE (Future Issues)

- **Issue #2 — "Cardy's Big Plan":** Cardy learns that plans are great, but sometimes you have to adjust when things don't go as expected. (Lesson: Flexibility)
- **Issue #3 — "Dome-ster's Big Idea":** The team works together to solve a problem before the big game (a broken zamboni, a missing jersey). (Lesson: Teamwork)
- **Issue #4 — "Chuggy Never Quits":** Chuggy is the slowest skater on the ice but teaches everyone that effort matters more than speed. (Lesson: Persistence)
- **Issue #5 — "The New Kid":** A new mascot arrives (great spot to introduce a Season Two expansion team) and the Founding Four help them feel welcome. (Lesson: Kindness/Inclusion)
