About PuckGold

LONG LIVE THE ICE.

PuckGoldBiz is here to modernize and supercharge hockey — Founding Four pace, PGB stakes, and fan-first energy.

Built for fans who already love it — and everyone just getting started.

Our mission is to build on and complement the best of pro hockey and take it to new levels of excitement and engagement. With Founding Four markets and an expanding First 32 wave, PuckGold brings the game — and those who love to play and watch it — to new places.

Events revitalize the ice while helping bring new audiences in. Hockey is full of tradition. We intend to create new ones through an innovative approach: running-clock Banana Hockey, fan aura windows, and clip-ready overtime. We’ll complement this with Fan Zone, Media Hub, and programs designed to change young lives for the better.

Inspired by the clarity of About LIV Golf — translated for the coldest game on earth.
