FAQs

How does the Advertise Affiliate Program work?

Refer advertisers to PuckGold Advertise / Launch with your tracked link. When they spend on campaigns, you earn a percentage of eligible ad spend based on your tier (up to 30%).

Is this the same as PuckGold Affiliates (shop / League Pass)?

No. Consumer Affiliates pay on fan purchases. This program pays on B2B advertiser acquisition and spend. You can join both via Talent Community.

How often are commissions paid?

Monthly, after validation of referred spend — once the partner payout portal is live. Until then, email affiliates@puckgold.com.

Do I need a PuckGold account to join?

Yes — sign up through the Talent Community and select Affiliate (or Brand / partnerships). We’ll route Advertise Affiliate applicants separately.

Is there a limit to how much I can earn?

No hard cap. Milestone bonuses unlock at higher referred spend. Fraudulent or self-referred spend does not pay.
