# Do & don’t

Visual grid: `visuals/PGB_Do-Dont.png`

## Do

- Use official files from this kit (SVG preferred, then transparent PNG).
- Keep clear space and minimum size.
- Choose the colorway that contrasts with the background.
- Use the **icon** when space is tight.
- Scale proportionally from a corner handle.

## Don’t

- Don’t stretch, squish, skew, or rotate the logo off-axis.
- Don’t recolor with off-palette hues (no purple gradients, neon glows, rainbow fills).
- Don’t add drop shadows, outlines, bevels, chrome filters, or glow.
- Don’t crop into the stars, tower, or letterforms.
- Don’t place the logo in a competing shape that becomes a new “badge.”
- Don’t typeset “PGB” or “PuckGold” as a fake logo in a random font.
- Don’t lock up PGB with another brand into a single fused mark (see co-marketing).
