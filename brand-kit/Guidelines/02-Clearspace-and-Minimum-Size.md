# Clear space & minimum size

See visuals: `visuals/PGB_Clearspace.png` · `visuals/PGB_Minimum-Size.png`

## Clear space

Leave empty padding equal to **x ≈ 1/5 of the logo’s height** on all sides.

- No type, icons, edges, or other logos inside the clear-space box.
- For co-marketing, also keep at least **2x** between PGB and the partner mark.

## Minimum size

| Context | Primary / Horizontal | Icon only |
|---------|----------------------|-----------|
| Digital | **48px** tall minimum | **24px** |
| Preferred digital | **120px+** tall | **48px+** |
| Print | **0.5 in** tall | **0.35 in** |

If the primary mark would fall below minimum, switch to the **icon**.

## Contrast

- On busy photos: place logo on a solid plate, gradient fade, or use a mono version with stronger contrast.
- Never place gold on champagne or platinum on white without checking contrast.
