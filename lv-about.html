LockerVision

ABOUT US

PuckGold LockerVision gives fans a look into every Founding Four locker — for every game and every player. It’s the destination for uniform schedules, kit design details, jersey + pads + helmet layers, and ice-rink stages.

What started as game-night outfit reveals is growing into a full hockey kit browser — Home Ice, Road White, Alternate Night, Classic Throwback, and Goalie Pad Sets — with DROP folders ready for photography and 3D renders.

Built for hockey first: jerseys, pads, helmets, gloves, skates — and the sheet — so every Founding Four look reads arena-ready.

FAQs

How many kits does each Founding Four team have?

Five edition lanes: Home Ice, Road White, Alternate Night, Classic Throwback, and a Goalie Pad Set. Named game outfits (e.g. Neon Reef Home) map into those editions on the schedule.

Why show pads and helmets — not just jerseys?

Hockey outfitting is a full kit. LockerVision stages jersey, pants/socks, pads, helmet, gloves, and skates — plus crease gear for goalies — so fans and merch partners see the whole look.

What’s the ice rink panel?

Each game and outfit page includes a rink schematic (and optional photo drop) for the venue — center ice, creases, and team mark.

Where do I drop finished art?

assets/teams/{slug}/05-Jerseys-Uniforms/{edition}/ for hero + layer PNGs. Per-game overrides in assets/vault/games/outfits/{gameid}/. Rinks in assets/vault/rinks/.
