## 5. COMPETITION FORMAT

**Stage One — Round Robin (6 games):** Every team plays every other team once.

**Points System**
| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Regulation Win | 3 |
| OT/SO Win | 2 |
| OT/SO Loss | 1 |
| Regulation Loss | 0 |

**Stage Two — Semifinals:** #1 vs #4, #2 vs #3.

**Stage Three — The Golden Final:** Best-of-two, aggregate goals, sudden-death OT if tied.

**Tiebreakers (in order):** head-to-head → goal differential → goals scored → fewest goals allowed → shootout.

## 9. TEAM NOTES

**🦎 Miami Mighty Geckz** — Finished #3 seed via tiebreaker over Chattanooga. Alternated a tough OT loss and a statement win in round robin. Upset Washington in the semifinal, then completed a 3-1 aggregate comeback to win the Cup in Game Two overtime. Home crowd at Neon Reef Stadium has been the loudest in the league through Weekend One.

**🐦 McLean Cardinals** — Only unbeaten team in the round robin (3-0). Dominant Game One of the Final (3-1) built a two-goal cushion that ultimately wasn't enough. Elite defensive numbers all weekend; still finishes with the tournament's Best Goaltender award.

**🏛️ Washington Whoompt** — Locked the #2 seed with a clean, efficient round robin. Lost a tight, well-played semifinal in overtime — the tournament's first signature "so close" storyline.

**🚂 Chattanooga Choo Choo** — Winless in the round robin but never outworked; wins the tournament's Sportsmanship Award and the fanbase's affection. Building block for a Season Two run.
