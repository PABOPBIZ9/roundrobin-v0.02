AFFILIATE QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Official PuckGoldBiz Affiliates FAQs — League Pass, Fan Zone, and Banana Hockey drops.

Who can apply?

Publishers, creators, streamers, clippers, community hosts, bloggers, and media entities. Individuals should be 18+, promote appropriate content, and preferably have 1,000+ followers on a primary channel (exceptions for strong community / niche ops). Apply via the Talent Community.

How much can I earn?

Launch draft: 15% on a new fan’s first qualifying purchase and 8% on other qualifying purchases in-window. Volume partners may unlock bonuses. See Earn Cash.

How long does the tracking cookie last?

7 days. If a fan clicks your link and purchases within those 168 hours, you get credit. A new click restarts the window.

Is this the same as Fan Zone referrals?

No. Fan Zone / promos referrals are member rewards. Affiliates is the creator/publisher commission program with tracked links. Don’t convert personal invite codes into affiliate links expecting double pay.

How do I get paid?

Via the partner payout portal once approved (bank / PayPal). Tax forms required before first payout. Until the portal is live, email affiliates@puckgold.com.

Can I join outside the US?

Yes. Contracts are in USD; exchange fees may apply. International partners will upload W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E before payout when the portal is live.

I want to own a team / franchise — is that affiliates?

That’s the owner-operator track. Use Franchise / Apply (and Advertise lead gen if you’re running awareness first). Affiliates is for content + traffic commissions.

I was accepted — what now?

Generate links, create content, share with your audience. Start with Generate Links and Create Content.

Other questions?

Email affiliates@puckgold.com · brands use Advertise / Launch · talent ops use Talent Community.
