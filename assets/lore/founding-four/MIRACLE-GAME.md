# THE MIRACLE GAME
## Golden Final · Game Two · Neon Reef Stadium

*Period-by-period — the night Season One became lore*

**Series:** McLean Cardinals vs. Miami Mighty Geckz  
**Entering Game 2:** McLean leads aggregate **3–1**  
**Leaving Game 2:** Miami wins **5–4 (OT)** · aggregate **6–5** · first-ever PuckGold Cup

---

### First period — The Cup feels decided
McLean doesn't blink. Two power-play goals. Game score 2–0 Cardinals. Aggregate **5–1**. Neon Reef goes quiet for the first time all tournament. Broadcast booths start writing coronation copy.

### Second period — A pulse
Miami power-play goal. Converted penalty shot. Game 3–2 McLean. Aggregate still **6–3**. By any reasonable measure, finished. By Miami measure: alive.

### Third period — The period Season One is remembered for
Three unanswered Geckz goals. Building shaking louder with every one. Game tied 4–4. Aggregate knotted **6–6** with under a minute left. McLean nearly wins it in regulation. They don't.

### Overtime — Triple-deke, top shelf
McLean presses. Shot blocked. Rebound springs Miami on a clean breakaway. Signature move of the tournament: **triple-deke, buried top shelf.**

Golden confetti. First champions: **Miami Mighty Geckz**.
