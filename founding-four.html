Chapter 03

Tournament Format & Rules

Stage One — Round Robin Qualifier: Each of the four teams plays the other three exactly once (6 games total), establishing seeding for the playoffs.

Stage Two — Semifinal Knockout: #1 vs #4, #2 vs #3. Winners advance to the Championship.

Stage Three — The Golden Final: Best-of-two aggregate-goals series. Higher combined goal total wins the PuckGold Cup. If aggregate is tied after Game Two → sudden-death overtime.

Chapter 07

The Championship Series — Full Recap

Semifinals

McLean 6, Chattanooga 2 — Depth and discipline overwhelm a tired Choo Choo squad; Cardinals cruise into the final.

Miami 3, Washington 2 (OT) — #3 shocks #2 on a clutch breakaway. The Geckz are going to the Final.

Golden Final — Best-of-Two Aggregate

Game One — Cardinal Perch Gardens: Clinical McLean defense. McLean 3, Miami 1. Aggregate 3–1 Empire. A two-goal mountain for Miami on the road.

Game Two — Neon Reef — “The Miracle Game”

1st: McLean two PP goals · 2–0 game · aggregate 5–1 · Geckz look finished.
2nd: Miami PP + penalty shot · 3–2 McLean · aggregate 6–3 · alive.
3rd — The Comeback: Three unanswered · 4–4 · aggregate 6–6 · building erupting.
OT: Rebound breakaway · signature triple-deke · top shelf.

FINAL: Miami 5, McLean 4 (OT) · Aggregate Miami 6–5 · First-ever PuckGold Champions.
