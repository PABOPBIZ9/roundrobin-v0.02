The Field

Four franchises walked into arenas that had never hosted a game. McLean — the Empire — disciplined and defense-first. Washington Whoomp! — tactical and quiet. Miami Mighty Geckz — fast, flashy, unproven. Chattanooga Choo Choo — blue-collar heart, everybody's second favorite.

Nobody had played a single competitive game as a franchise. By Sunday night, one of them had a trophy.

Round Robin — The Empire Looks Unbeatable

McLean announced itself immediately: 4–1 at Terminal Arena, 5–2 at Cardinal Perch Gardens, closed the robin 3–0. Nine goals for, five against. It felt like a coronation.

Washington locked the two-seed the hard way — OT over Miami, then 4–3 over Chattanooga. Miami needed a miracle just to survive Game 6 (a 3–2 loss to McLean) and snuck into the #3 seed on goal differential. Chattanooga left with 0 points and all the fans' hearts.

Semifinals — One Chalk, One Chaos

McLean 6, Chattanooga 2 went as advertised. Depth wore down a Choo Choo club that had left everything on the ice in the robin.

Miami 3, Washington 2 (OT) was the first gut-punch. #3 knocked out #2 on a clutch breakaway. Nobody expected what came next to be bigger.

The Miracle Game

Game One at Cardinal Perch: McLean 3, Miami 1. Aggregate 3–1 Empire. Clinical. Suffocating. Done — until Neon Reef.

1st: McLean up 2–0 · aggregate 5–1 · Cup feels decided.
2nd: Miami PP + penalty shot · still 6–3 down · alive.
3rd: Three unanswered · 4–4 game · 6–6 aggregate · building erupting.
OT: Rebound breakaway · triple-deke · top shelf.

FINAL: Miami 5, McLean 4 (OT) · Aggregate Miami 6–5 · First-ever PuckGold Champions.
