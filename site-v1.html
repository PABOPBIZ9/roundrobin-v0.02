Virtual Venues

Take the tour.

Every Founding Four team plays home games in a stadium built entirely around its identity.

NEON REEF STADIUM

Neon Reef Stadium

Home of the Miami Mighty Geckz

An open-air, oceanfront rink built for a party. Neon-lit boards throw turquoise and pink light across the ice, palm trees ring the upper concourse, and a yacht dock sits just beyond the glass.

Oceanfront iceNeon boardsYacht dockNight games
CARDINAL PERCH GARDENS

Cardinal Perch Gardens

Home of the McLean Cardinals

A refined arena built into a formal park, framed by ancient oak trees and sharp red-glass architecture. Tiered stone terraces and a championship plaza greet fans at the gate — everything here says dynasty.

Formal gardensGlass architectureChampionship plaza
THE DISTRICT DOME

The District Dome

Home of Washington Whoomp!

A monumental domed arena inspired by classic civic architecture — grand columns, a glass roof patterned with stars, and sightlines to a reflecting pool outside. A 360-degree scoreboard hangs at center ice.

Domed roof360° scoreboardReflecting pool views
TERMINAL ARENA

Terminal Arena

Home of the Chattanooga Choo Choo

A converted historic train station turned hockey barn — exposed brick, heavy timber trusses, and arched windows overlooking a vintage locomotive. The goal horn is modeled on a steam-engine whistle.

Converted train stationSteam-whistle hornClock tower
